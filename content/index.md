I work on how machines can read human intent from the body: the electrical activity of muscles (surface EMG), movement kinematics, posture and gait.

At the moment I'm writing my M.Sc. thesis in Computational Neuroscience (LMU Munich) at ETH Zurich, comparing machine-learning algorithms that decode hand gestures from high-density surface EMG. Before that I studied sport science at TU Munich and ran human–robot handover experiments built around two questions: how does a person respond to the way a robot moves, and how should the robot move so the person understands it?

**I'm looking for industry roles in human–machine, human–computer and human–robot interaction (HMI, HCI, HRI).** If that fits your team, I'd be glad to hear from you: the email link is at the bottom of every page.

Here you'll find my [projects and publications](/projects/), more [about me](/about/), and [How systems interact](/wiki/), a small wiki on how brains, bodies and machines communicate.
