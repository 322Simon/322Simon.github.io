When a person and a machine work together, the interesting object is neither one of them. The same goes for a brain and a muscle, or two people carrying a table.

Brains talk to muscles, muscles to machines, people to robots. Each entry in this small wiki explains one of these interactions: who sends the signal, how it travels, and what happens when it gets lost on the way. Written for curious people, not reviewers.

Entries link to each other, like a small Wikipedia, and get updated as I learn more. The map below shows how they connect.
