I came to computational neuroscience from the movement side: sport science at TU Munich with a focus on neural mechanisms and human movement computation, and now an M.Sc. in Neuroscience at LMU Munich.

That background shapes how I work. I'm at home on the human side of the problem: designing studies, recording clean signals from people, running experiments with haptic robots. My master's thesis is where that meets machine learning.

Whatever the setup, I look for holistic solutions: ones that work for every system involved, the person as much as the machine.

## Education

  Thesis at ETH Zurich: *Evaluation of different Machine Learning Algorithms for Gesture Decoding via EMG* (2026 – 2027)
- **B.Sc. Sport Science**, Technical University of Munich · 2022 – 2025
  Thesis: *Predicting Object Weight Using Robot Kinematic Cues in Human-Robot Handovers*

## Research experience
